02 — what it does02 — 功能详解

Everything you need, from first dit to confident copy 从第一个“嘀”到稳定抄收，一应俱全

Learn, drills, simulated QSOs, reference, radio tools and local progress, all in one focused trainer. 学习、练习、模拟通联、参考资料、无线电工具与本地进度，集中在一款专注的训练器中。

Learn学习

A real course, not a flashcard deck一套真正的课程，而不只是闪卡

MorseCQ teaches the way experienced operators recommend: characters at full speed from day one, new ones added only when the evidence says you copy the current set reliably.MorseCQ 按资深报务员推荐的方式教学：从第一天起就以全速发音学字符，只有当逐字符的表现证明你已稳定抄收，才加入新字符。

  • A gentle first lesson: hear dit and dah, then K and M, with guided recognition and sending before you copy on your own.平缓的第一课：先听“嘀”和“嗒”，再学 K 和 M，有引导地辨认与拍发之后再独立抄收。
  • Koch method: 42 lessons that advance through challenges, with per-character evidence.Koch 教学法：42 课，通过挑战推进，依据逐字符的掌握情况。
  • Farnsworth spacing: fast characters, generous gaps while you learn.Farnsworth 间距：字符快速，字间留足思考时间。
  • Spaced review and a mistake notebook: characters you miss come back when they are due.间隔复习与错题本：抄错的字符会在到期时再次出现。
  • A daily plan and streaks keep a little practice going every day.每日计划与连续打卡，每天都练一点。

Receive & send drills抄收与拍发练习

Copy by ear, key by hand用耳朵抄收，用手拍发

Copy drills cover everything you hear on the air:抄收练习覆盖你在空中会听到的一切：

Character groups字符组 Single characters单字符 Words & sentences单词与句子 Abbreviations & Q-codes缩略语与 Q 简语 Number groups数字组 Callsigns呼号 Look-alike pairs易混字符对 Short QSOs简短通联 Contest exchanges比赛交换

Send practice works on an on-screen straight key or iambic paddle, on the keyboard and with USB keyer adapters. A streaming decoder shows what you keyed in real time, and the summary tells you whether your dahs are too short or your gaps too long.拍发练习支持屏幕上的手键、双桨自动键（iambic）、键盘以及 USB 电键适配器。流式解码器实时显示你拍出的内容，练习小结会告诉你“嗒”是否太短、间隔是否太长。

Toward the air走向上机

Rehearse your first QSO before you make it真正上机之前，先把第一次通联练熟

When the alphabet is in your ears, MorseCQ keeps going: goal routes for a first QSO, ragchew conversation or contesting, and a simulator that plays the other station.字母听熟之后，MorseCQ 继续带你走：第一次通联、日常闲聊、比赛三条目标路线，以及一个扮演对方电台的模拟器。

  • Simulated QSOs with readiness hints, corrections and targeted repetition.模拟通联，带准备度提示、纠错与针对性重复。
  • Contest and park-to-park POTA exchanges, at the pace of a real pile-up.比赛与公园对公园（POTA）交换，节奏贴近真实。
  • Whole-word and sentence comprehension, following CW Academy intermediate directions.整词与整句理解，参照 CW Academy 中级课程方向。
  • Assisted attempts stay separate from independent mastery, so your stats stay honest.辅助练习与独立掌握分开统计，进度数据真实可信。

Listen by microphone麦克风听码

Decode Morse from live audio, such as a radio, a practice oscillator or a video, or work through a recording in the audio workbench.从现场音频解码摩尔斯电码，电台、练习振荡器或视频都行；也可以在音频工作台里逐段分析录音。

Reference & translator参考资料与翻译器

Alphabet, punctuation, prosigns, Q-codes and CW abbreviations, plus a two-way text ↔ Morse translator with Chinese telegraph codes.字母、标点、勤务符号、Q 简语和 CW 缩略语，外加支持中文电码的文本 ↔ 摩尔斯双向翻译器。

Radio tools无线电工具

Maidenhead locator with distance and beam heading, IARU band edges with antenna lengths, a CW speed calculator, RST builder and UTC clock.梅登黑德网格定位（含距离与方位角）、IARU 频段边界（含天线长度）、CW 速度换算、RST 报告生成器和 UTC 时钟。

Statistics统计

Accuracy trends, a per-character grid, a confusion heat-map of the characters you mix up, and a practice calendar.准确率趋势、逐字符掌握度网格、显示你常混淆哪些字符的热力图，以及练习日历。

Five looks, ten languages五种风格，十种语言

Modern Calm, Classic Brass, Night Radio, Paper Handbook and Fresh Cartoon, each light or dark, in ten interface languages.Modern Calm、Classic Brass、Night Radio、Paper Handbook、Fresh Cartoon，每种都有浅色与深色，界面支持十种语言。

Sound, haptics, light声音、振动、闪光

Low-latency sidetone on every platform, haptics on phones and a screen flash everywhere, with your own speed, tone and spacing.全平台低延迟侧音、手机振动反馈、所有平台的屏幕闪光，速度、音调与间距随你设置。

Your progress, your device你的进度，保存在本机

Progress, materials and recordings stay on your device. Clear them any time from Me; no personal data is collected.进度、素材与录音保存在本机，可随时从“我”页面清除；不收集任何个人数据。

At home on the desktop桌面端体验完整

Window size remembered, close to tray, a tray menu and keyboard shortcuts on macOS, Windows and Linux.在 macOS、Windows、Linux 上记住窗口大小，支持关闭到托盘、托盘菜单和快捷键。

a practice round一次练习

How a practice round works一次练习是怎么进行的

  1. 1

    You key it你拍发

    Type, tap a straight key, squeeze a paddle or plug in a USB keyer. MorseCQ decodes your keying into text.打字、点手键、按双桨电键或接上 USB 电键，MorseCQ 把你的拍发解码成文字。

  2. 2

    MorseCQ evaluates itMorseCQ 评估

    The trainer checks timing, accuracy and rhythm locally, then records the result in your on-device progress.训练器在本机检查节奏、准确率与拍发质量，再把结果写入本地进度。

  3. 3

    You hear it你听到反馈

    Replay the prompt and your own keying at the speed and tone you choose, then try again.按你选择的速度和音调回放题目与自己的拍发，然后继续练习。

07 — faq07 — 常见问题

Questions, answered常见问题

Do I need an amateur radio licence?需要业余无线电执照吗？

No. MorseCQ never transmits anything, so anyone can learn. If you are a licensed operator, it is a good way to keep your CW sharp between contacts.不需要。MorseCQ 不会发射任何信号，任何人都可以学习。如果你持有执照，它也是在两次上机之间保持 CW 手感的好办法。

Do I need an account or an internet connection?需要账号或联网吗？

Neither. Open the app and start learning. Lessons, drills, materials and progress all run on your device.都不需要。打开应用即可开始学习，课程、练习、素材与进度都在本机运行。

Where is Morse chat?摩尔斯聊天在哪里？

MorseCQ is the offline trainer and has no chat or calls. Morse chat lives in DitMesh, a separate app.MorseCQ 是离线训练器，不含聊天或通话。摩尔斯聊天请使用独立应用 DitMesh。

What happens to my data?我的数据会怎样处理？

It stays on your device. MorseCQ collects no personal data and has no analytics or advertising. Me → Clear learning data removes progress, materials and recordings. See the privacy policy.数据只保存在你的设备上。MorseCQ 不收集任何个人数据，没有统计和广告。“我 → 清除学习数据”可删除进度、素材与录音。详见隐私政策。

Why does my system warn me when I install it?为什么安装时系统会弹出警告？

The 1.0.1 builds are not code-signed or notarized yet. The install guide shows how to confirm on each platform. You can check any download against SHA256SUMS.1.0.1 安装包尚未代码签名或公证。安装指南说明了各平台如何确认。可用 SHA256SUMS 校验任何下载文件。

Which languages does the app speak?应用支持哪些语言？

Ten: English, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese and Russian. The translator also handles Chinese telegraph codes.十种：英语、简体中文、繁体中文、日语、韩语、德语、法语、西班牙语、葡萄牙语和俄语。翻译器还支持中文电码。

Is it really free?真的免费吗？

Yes. MorseCQ is free software under the GNU General Public License v3.0. Bug reports and contributions are welcome on GitHub.是的。MorseCQ 是遵循 GNU GPL v3.0 的自由软件，欢迎到 GitHub 提交问题和贡献代码。
